# IronMaze — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

IronMaze ("the game") is a tower defense game for Android, published on Google Play. This policy explains what
data is involved when you play, and what your rights are.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Samuel Engström**, Finland, the developer of the game.
Contact: **ironmazegame@gmail.com**. You will get an answer within one month.

## What the game itself collects

**Nothing.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and the developer
runs no servers. Your progress (Tech Cores, Scrap, unlocks, best waves, settings) is stored **only on your
device**, encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore. The developer never receives it.

## Google services used by the game

The game uses the following Google services. Google processes their data under its own policies, as a separate
controller.

- **Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads).**
  - Ads are shown **only when you choose** to watch one: to revive a run, or to earn 2 Tech Cores in the Store.
    There are no banners, no interstitials and no forced ads.
  - So that an ad is ready when you ask for one, the game loads ads in the background from the moment it
    starts. Google therefore receives data even if you never watch an ad: your device's **advertising ID** and
    app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), ad interactions, and
    diagnostic data.
  - Google uses this data to deliver, measure and, where allowed, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Google
    may share some of it with advertising partners that take part in its ad auction.
- **Google Play Billing (optional in-app purchases).**
  - Google processes your payment.
  - The game receives a purchase record (product, order ID and purchase token), checks it on your device and
    grants the items.
  - It keeps the order ID on your device only, so that a purchase is never granted twice.
- **Google Play age signals.**
  - When the game starts, it asks Google Play for your age range. Where the law requires it (for example in
    Texas and Brazil), Play may provide it. Elsewhere, depending on your Google Play settings, Play may first ask
    whether to share your age range with the game. You can decline, and the game works the same.
  - The game uses the answer only to make ads suitable for younger players (see "Younger players"). It keeps only
    the resulting age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or other) in memory while the game runs, and never stores or
    logs it. Ad requests carry the resulting ad settings, not your age range.

Learn more: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) ·
[How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis under the GDPR |
|---|---|
| Personalised ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland | Your consent (Article 6(1)(a)) |
| Non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention | Legitimate interests: keeping the game free and safe (Article 6(1)(f)) |
| Purchases | Performance of a contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)) |
| Age signals | Legitimate interests: protecting younger players (Article 6(1)(f)) |

You do not have to provide any data to play. Ads and purchases are optional, but ads load in the background as
described above. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means.

## Consent and your choices

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form (UMP) before
  personalised ads can be shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in **Settings → Account &
  data → Privacy choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Privacy → Ads).
- You never have to watch an ad or buy anything to progress: every turret, tier and perk can be earned by playing.

## Younger players

The game is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

When the game starts, it asks Google Play for your age range (see "Google Play age signals"). Then:

- **Under 13:** the game asks Google for child-directed ads, which are never personalised, rated for all ages.
- **13 to 17, or your age must be verified where the law requires it:** the game asks Google for
  non-personalised ads suitable for teens, and tells Google's consent form that you are under the age of
  consent.
- **Anything else, or no answer from Play within 3 seconds:** ads are the same as for every other player. In the
  EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised only with your consent. Elsewhere, Google may personalise
  them unless you turn personalised ads off in your device's settings (Privacy → Ads).

## International transfers

Google may process data outside the EEA, for example in the United States. For these transfers, Google relies on
the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress and purchased Tech Cores are stored **on this device only**. There is no cloud save: they are not
backed up and cannot be moved to another device. They stay until you delete them (**Settings → Account & data →
Delete local data**) or uninstall the game, and then they are gone for good.

There is no account and no server copy, so the developer has nothing else to delete. Google keeps ad and
purchase data as described in its privacy policy; purchase history stays in your Google account. The Supporter
pack, a one-time purchase, can be restored on any device with **Restore purchases**.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for:

- access to your data
- correction of your data
- erasure of your data
- restriction of its processing
- data portability

You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests. The developer holds no data about you, so a
request to the developer can only confirm that. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy tools at
[myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

You can complain to a data protection authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection
Ombudsman ([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi)), or you can contact the authority where you live.

## Security

Saved progress is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a device-bound key. All network traffic (billing and ads only) uses
HTTPS.
